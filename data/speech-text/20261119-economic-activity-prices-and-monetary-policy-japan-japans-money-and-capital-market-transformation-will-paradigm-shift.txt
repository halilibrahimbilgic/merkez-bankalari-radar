 I. Economic Activity and Prices&nbsp;

 I will begin with developments in economic activity and prices.&nbsp;

 Overseas economies have grown moderately on the whole. Regarding the U.S. economy,&nbsp;despite initial concerns that domestic demand might decline due to the impact of wideranging tariffs imposed in April 2025 by the Trump administration, the actual impact has been&nbsp;limited. Specifically, although there was a slowdown in U.S. employment in 2025, it has been&nbsp;heading toward improvement in 2026, and corporate profits for IT-related firms in particular&nbsp;have also improved. In 2025, in light of the slowdown in employment, the Federal Reserve&nbsp;cut its policy interest rate in September, October, and December (Chart 1). While the Federal&nbsp;Open Market Committee (FOMC) participants' projections for the federal funds rate&nbsp;suggested further rate cuts as of the beginning of 2026, since the middle of 2026 the&nbsp;projections have shifted to suggesting potential rate hikes. The Federal Reserve did in fact&nbsp;raise the rate in September.
